---
title: Catch-all mailboxes
description: Accept-all recipient behavior and operational tradeoffs.
section: Reference
tags: [Mailbox, Operations]
---

A catch-all configuration accepts mail for otherwise undefined addresses at a domain. It can prevent missed messages caused by address mistakes, but it also attracts spam, makes recipient validation harder and can hide configuration errors.

Use catch-all behavior deliberately and monitor the resulting mailbox rather than treating it as a substitute for managing aliases.
