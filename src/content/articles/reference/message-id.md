---
title: Message-ID
description: The role of Message-ID in identifying and correlating email messages.
section: Reference
tags: [Headers]
---

`Message-ID` is a globally intended message identifier generated by a mail client or sending system. It is useful for tracing, threading and correlating logs across systems.

It is not a cryptographic identity and should not be treated as proof of authorship. Duplicate, malformed or missing identifiers can also occur.
