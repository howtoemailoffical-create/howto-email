---
title: Role-based email addresses
description: Addresses such as postmaster and abuse represent functions rather than individual people.
section: Reference
tags: [Addressing, Operations, Standards]
---

Role addresses provide stable contact points for operational functions.

`postmaster@` has a special place in SMTP operations, and `abuse@` is widely standardized for reporting inappropriate public behavior from a domain or network.

Route role addresses somewhere monitored. Publishing or accepting them without anybody reading them defeats their purpose.
