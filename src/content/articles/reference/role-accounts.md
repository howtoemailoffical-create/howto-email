---
title: Postmaster and abuse mailboxes
description: Operational role addresses used around Internet mail domains.
section: Reference
tags: [Operations, Standards]
---

Internet mail operations historically define conventional role addresses such as `postmaster` for mail-system issues and `abuse` for abuse reporting. Providers and standards may impose expectations around reachability for certain roles.

Organizations should route these addresses to monitored workflows rather than creating unattended mailboxes.
