Large mailbox providers may expose dashboards for qualified senders showing signals such as authentication, reputation, spam complaints or delivery errors.

These dashboards are provider-specific views of traffic that provider sees. They are not a universal measurement of your reputation everywhere on the Internet.

Use them alongside your own SMTP logs, bounce processing, complaint data and DMARC reporting.
