---
title: Retention is not backup
description: Why compliance retention, mailbox recovery and disaster recovery are different controls.
section: Reference
tags: [Retention, Backup, Operations]
---

Retention determines how long information must or may remain available under a policy.

Backup creates recoverable copies for failures or loss.

They can overlap, but they solve different problems. A retention system optimized for legal discovery is not automatically the right disaster-recovery mechanism, and a backup does not automatically satisfy a records-retention obligation.

Define requirements first, then map the platform controls to them.
