---
title: Read receipts and disposition notifications
description: Why a requested read receipt is not reliable proof a person read a message.
section: Reference
tags: [Headers, Clients, Privacy]
---

Message Disposition Notifications can request information about what a recipient's mail client did with a message.

The recipient, client or organization may refuse to send one. Automated processing can also make "displayed" semantics different from human understanding.

Treat read receipts as optional workflow signals, not legal or cryptographic proof that a person read and understood a message.
