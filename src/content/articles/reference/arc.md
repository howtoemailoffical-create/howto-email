---
title: ARC
description: Authenticated Received Chain and authentication across intermediaries.
section: Reference
tags: [ARC, Forwarding]
---

ARC lets participating intermediaries preserve authentication observations and seal a chain as messages pass through forwarding or list systems that may alter authentication outcomes.

ARC does not replace SPF, DKIM or DMARC. Receivers decide whether an ARC chain and its sealers are trustworthy enough to influence local disposition.
