---
title: Delivery Status Notifications
description: How email systems report delivery failures and status information.
section: Reference
tags: [SMTP, Bounces]
---

Delivery Status Notifications describe message delivery outcomes in a machine-readable MIME format. They can include the original recipient, final recipient, action, status and diagnostic code.

A DSN is more useful than a generic 'bounce' label because it preserves the remote system's classification and diagnostic evidence.
