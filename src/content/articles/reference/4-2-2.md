---
title: Enhanced status 4.2.2
description: A temporary mailbox-full condition should normally be retried rather than treated as an invalid address.
section: Reference
tags: [SMTP, Bounces, Status Codes]
---

`4.2.2` indicates the mailbox is full under the enhanced status-code model.

Because it is a 4.x.x condition, the sender should normally queue and retry.

If it persists until the sender's queue lifetime expires, the sender can eventually return a delivery failure.

Do not immediately suppress the address as nonexistent.
