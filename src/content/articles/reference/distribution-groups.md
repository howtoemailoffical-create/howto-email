A distribution group represents multiple recipients behind one address.

Expansion can occur inside a hosted mail platform, directory, mailing-list manager or gateway.

## Operational questions

Know who can send to the group, who can change membership, whether external senders are allowed, whether moderation exists and how bounces are handled.

Large groups can turn one compromised account into a high-impact internal phishing channel, so authorization matters.
