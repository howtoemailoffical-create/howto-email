---
title: IMAP and POP3
description: Mailbox access protocols and their common ports.
section: Reference
tags: [IMAP, POP3]
---

IMAP synchronizes mailbox state and folders with a server. Common ports are **143** with STARTTLS support and **993** for implicit TLS. POP3 is a simpler retrieval protocol, commonly using **110** or **995** with implicit TLS.

These protocols access stored mail; SMTP is used for sending and transfer.
