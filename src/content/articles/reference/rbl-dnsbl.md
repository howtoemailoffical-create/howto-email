---
title: DNS blocklists
description: How IP and domain reputation lists are queried and used.
section: Reference
tags: [Reputation, DNS]
---

DNS-based blocklists publish reputation or abuse listings that receiving systems may query as one filtering signal. Listing criteria, removal processes and reliability vary widely.

A listing is not automatically proof that all mail from an IP is malicious. Operators should identify the specific list, reason and underlying abuse before pursuing delisting.
