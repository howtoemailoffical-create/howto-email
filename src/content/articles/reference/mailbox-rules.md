---
title: Mailbox rules
description: Server-side rules can automate mail handling—and hide attacker activity.
section: Reference
tags: [Mailbox, Security, Automation]
---

Mailbox rules can move, delete, forward or otherwise process messages based on conditions.

They are useful for normal workflow, but a compromised account can use rules to hide replies, suppress security notifications or forward sensitive conversations.

During account-compromise investigations, review rules along with delegates, forwarding and application grants.
