---
title: Enhanced status 5.1.1
description: A permanent addressing failure commonly associated with a nonexistent destination mailbox.
section: Reference
tags: [SMTP, Bounces, Status Codes]
---

`5.1.1` means the destination mailbox address does not exist under the enhanced status-code specification.

For a mailing list, suppress or correct the address rather than retrying it forever.

For business mail, verify the address spelling and whether the recipient was recently renamed or removed.

Always retain the receiver's accompanying text because provider diagnostics can add useful context.
