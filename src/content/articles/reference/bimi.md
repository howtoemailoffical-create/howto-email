---
title: BIMI
description: Brand Indicators for Message Identification and its authentication dependencies.
section: Reference
tags: [BIMI, DMARC]
---

BIMI allows participating mailbox providers to display a domain-controlled brand indicator when provider requirements are satisfied. It builds on strong domain authentication and DMARC enforcement and may involve verified mark credentials depending on provider requirements.

BIMI is a branding mechanism, not a replacement for anti-phishing controls.
