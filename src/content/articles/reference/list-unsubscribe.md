---
title: List-Unsubscribe headers
description: Headers used to expose mailing-list unsubscribe mechanisms.
section: Reference
tags: [Headers, Bulk Email]
---

`List-Unsubscribe` can advertise HTTP and mail-based unsubscribe methods. `List-Unsubscribe-Post` supports one-click unsubscribe behavior defined for participating senders and receivers.

These headers do not replace consent, preference management or applicable legal obligations. They provide standardized message metadata for unsubscribe actions.
