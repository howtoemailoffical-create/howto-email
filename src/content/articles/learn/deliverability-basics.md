Delivery means a receiving system accepted the message. Deliverability describes where that accepted message ultimately lands and whether users can reasonably receive it.

Authentication, domain and IP reputation, complaints, list quality, volume patterns, content and recipient behavior can all influence filtering.

## Diagnose before changing

Separate SMTP rejection from post-acceptance filtering. Compare affected and successful messages, inspect headers, establish which providers are affected, and avoid changing multiple variables at once.
