---
title: Mail flow logging
description: What useful SMTP and gateway logs should tell an operator.
section: Learn
tags: [Logging, Operations]
---

Useful mail logs let you correlate a message across receipt, filtering, queueing and delivery. Capture timestamps, queue or message identifiers, envelope sender and recipients, peer information, SMTP status, routing decisions and relevant security verdicts while respecting privacy requirements.

Consistent identifiers are critical: without them, a multi-hop incident becomes a manual timestamp-matching exercise.
