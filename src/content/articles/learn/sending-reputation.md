---
title: Sending reputation
description: How domain and IP reputation affect email acceptance and placement.
section: Learn
tags: [Deliverability, Reputation]
---

Receivers build reputation from observed sending behavior. Signals can include complaint rates, invalid recipients, volume consistency, authentication, historical traffic and abuse patterns.

Dedicated IPs give a sender more direct control over IP reputation but also require enough well-managed traffic to establish it. Shared services pool some infrastructure reputation while still evaluating domains and individual streams.

Reputation is earned over time; DNS records alone cannot manufacture it.
