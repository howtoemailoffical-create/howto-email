title: Bulk email foundations
description: The technical and operational pieces behind responsible high-volume sending.
section: Learn
tags: [Bulk Email, Deliverability]
---

Bulk sending is an operations problem as much as a protocol problem. Maintain permission and list hygiene, authenticate domains, make unsubscribe straightforward, separate traffic streams where useful, control volume, process bounces and complaints, and monitor provider feedback.

Requirements vary by receiving provider and jurisdiction, so high-volume programs should track current provider rules and applicable law rather than treating one checklist as permanent.
