Email DLP inspects messages for information an organization does not want sent in a particular way.

Policies can look at content, labels, attachments, sender/recipient context and other signals. Actions might audit, warn, block, quarantine, encrypt or require another control.

## False positives are an operational problem

A pattern that resembles a sensitive identifier is not necessarily sensitive data. Good DLP programs use context, testing, exceptions and incident review instead of turning on broad blocking rules and hoping for the best.

## Know where enforcement occurs

A cloud mail platform, secure email gateway and endpoint DLP product can all make different decisions. Document which layer owns which policy.
