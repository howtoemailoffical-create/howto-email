---
title: Transactional vs marketing email
description: Why separating message streams improves operations and deliverability.
section: Learn
tags: [Deliverability, Operations]
---

Transactional mail is triggered by a user or system event, such as a receipt or password reset. Marketing mail is promotional or campaign-oriented. Their urgency, consent model, volume pattern and complaint risk differ.

Separating streams with appropriate subdomains, DKIM selectors, infrastructure or provider configurations can make reputation and incident handling easier. It also reduces the chance that a marketing problem disrupts critical operational mail.
