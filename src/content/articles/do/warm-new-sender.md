---
title: Introduce a new sending domain or IP
description: Operational considerations for establishing a new outbound sending identity.
section: Do
tags: [Deliverability, Operations]
---

Authenticate the domain correctly before meaningful volume begins. Start with legitimate, expected recipients and stable sending patterns rather than immediately pushing maximum campaign volume.

Monitor bounces, complaints, provider feedback and authentication. Avoid artificial engagement schemes: reputation should reflect real wanted mail and clean recipient practices.
