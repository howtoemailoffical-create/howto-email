---
title: Hunt a malicious URL across email
description: Pivot from one reported message to the rest of the campaign.
section: Do
tags: [Threat Hunting, Phishing, Security]
---

Preserve the reported message and extract the original URL safely using approved tooling.

Search mail telemetry for the URL, registered domain and relevant rewritten/protection URL representations.

Expand with related indicators such as sender, Reply-To, subject, attachment and sending IP.

Identify recipients and determine whether messages were delivered, quarantined, clicked or otherwise acted upon where your authorized security tooling provides that data.

Do not visit a suspicious URL from a normal workstation just to see what it does.
