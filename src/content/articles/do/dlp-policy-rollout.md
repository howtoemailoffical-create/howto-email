---
title: Roll out an email DLP policy
description: Start with evidence and tuning before turning a broad detector into a production block.
section: Do
tags: [DLP, Security, Operations]
---

Define the data and business behavior the policy is supposed to protect.

Start in an audit/test mode where the platform supports it. Review matches with the data owners and security team.

Measure false positives and identify legitimate workflows that need a safer approved path.

Then introduce user warnings, justification, encryption or blocking according to risk.

Keep exceptions narrow, owned and periodically reviewed.
