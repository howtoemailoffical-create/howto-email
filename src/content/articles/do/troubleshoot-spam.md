---
title: Troubleshoot spam placement
description: Investigate accepted messages that land in junk.
section: Do
tags: [Deliverability, Troubleshooting]
---

Establish scope first: one mailbox, one provider, one campaign or all traffic? Compare a successful message with an affected one.

Check SPF, DKIM, DMARC and alignment; confirm the real sending infrastructure; review volume changes, complaints, invalid recipients, list acquisition and domain/IP reputation signals available from providers.

Change variables methodically so you can tell which change mattered.
