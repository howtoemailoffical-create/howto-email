---
title: Prepare a domain for bulk sending
description: Technical readiness before a new organization begins high-volume email.
section: Do
tags: [Bulk Email, DMARC]
---

Establish separate, owned sending domains or subdomains as appropriate; inventory platforms; configure SPF and DKIM; deploy DMARC reporting; and make sure visible From identities align.

Set up bounce and complaint handling, unsubscribe workflows, list acquisition rules and monitoring before scaling volume. Test representative messages with major receiving environments and keep transactional traffic operationally distinguishable from marketing.
