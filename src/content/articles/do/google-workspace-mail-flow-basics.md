---
title: Google Workspace mail flow checklist
description: A vendor-specific checklist for routing, gateways and domain authentication.
section: Do
tags: [Google Workspace, Operations]
---

Inventory domains, routing rules, inbound/outbound gateways, SMTP relay usage and third-party senders. Verify DKIM signing, SPF authorization and DMARC alignment using real received messages.

Routing or gateway changes can alter which system is responsible for filtering and authentication observations. Verify current Google documentation before changing production settings.
