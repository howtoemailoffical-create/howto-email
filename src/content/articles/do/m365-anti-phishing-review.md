---
title: Review Microsoft 365 anti-phishing configuration
description: Check impersonation and spoof protections as part of the broader mail-security posture.
section: Do
tags: [Microsoft 365, Phishing, Security]
---

Review the anti-phishing policies that apply to the intended users and domains.

Understand which users/domains are protected from impersonation, how mailbox intelligence or similar signals are used, and what actions occur on detections.

Then test representative legitimate and suspicious scenarios in a controlled way.

Avoid blanket trusted-sender exceptions as the first response to false positives. Determine which detection fired and whether the exception can be scoped more narrowly.
