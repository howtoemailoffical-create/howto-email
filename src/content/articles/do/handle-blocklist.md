---
title: Respond to an email blocklist listing
description: Fix the cause before requesting reputation delisting.
section: Do
tags: [Reputation, Incident Response]
---

Identify the exact IP or domain listed and the specific list. Investigate why it was listed: compromised accounts, open relay, malware, poor recipient acquisition or shared-provider abuse are materially different problems.

Contain and remediate the source first. Then follow the list operator's documented removal process if removal is necessary. Delisting without fixing the cause often leads to relisting.
