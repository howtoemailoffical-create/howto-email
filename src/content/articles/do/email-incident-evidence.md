---
title: Preserve evidence during an email incident
description: What to retain before messages, logs and cloud telemetry disappear.
section: Do
tags: [Incident Response, Logging, Security]
---

For a serious email incident, preserve evidence before making changes that overwrite or invalidate it.

Useful material can include:

- original raw messages;
- complete headers;
- message/queue identifiers;
- mail-flow or gateway trace results;
- sign-in and identity events;
- mailbox rules and forwarding;
- OAuth/application grants;
- relevant DNS state;
- affected URLs and attachment hashes;
- timestamps and actions taken by responders.

Follow your organization's legal, privacy and retention requirements. Do not copy sensitive message content into public tools just because it is convenient.
