---
title: Troubleshoot missing TLS-RPT reports
description: Verify policy publication and remember that reporting depends on participating senders.
section: Do
tags: [TLS-RPT, Reporting, Troubleshooting]
---

Query the TXT record at:

```text
_smtp._tls.example.com
```

Confirm the syntax and reporting URI are correct and that the destination can receive reports.

Then check spam/quarantine, mailbox or HTTPS endpoint logs, and any report processor.

A lack of reports does not prove nobody attempted delivery or that TLS is perfect. TLS-RPT is generated by participating sending systems and reporting behavior varies.

## Reference material

- [RFC 8460 — SMTP TLS Reporting](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8460)
